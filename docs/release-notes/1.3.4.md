## Kannu 1.3.4 — Heimdall

The clipboard panel works again, and the notch comes back after your Mac sleeps or locks.

Requires macOS 14.6 or later (unchanged since 1.2.0). Existing installs update in place.

---

### The clipboard panel shows its content again

Since 1.3.0, opening the clipboard panel showed the wrong part of it: the header with the tabs and
the search field sat above the top of the panel, and the list was pushed out of view. It looked
blank or broken, on the desktop and over fullscreen apps alike. It now opens whole, on the screen
you are looking at.

- **It opens on the screen under your pointer.** With more than one display it used to open on the
  screen of whichever window had focus.
- **A saved position is only reused if the panel would actually be visible there.** A spot remembered
  on a display that has since moved or gone no longer strands the panel off-screen. It re-centres
  instead. Your previously saved position is ignored once after updating; drag the panel where you
  want it and that position sticks.

### The notch stays with you after sleep or lock

After a sleep or a lock, macOS could drop the notch from every fullscreen space except one. It kept
running, but was missing in your other fullscreen apps until you relaunched Kannu. Kannu now notices
this when you switch spaces, wake or unlock, and puts the notch back.

### Quit is easy to find

**Settings › General** now has a **Quit Kannu** row. Searching Settings for "quit" or "exit" takes
you straight to it. It used to be a small button in the window's toolbar that search couldn't find.

### Panels stay put when you click elsewhere

Kannu's floating panels (the clipboard, music and timer controls, and the screen assistant) are now
all set up the same way, so none of them can disappear just because you clicked into another app.

Everything else is unchanged.
