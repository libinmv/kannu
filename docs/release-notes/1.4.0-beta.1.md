## Kannu 1.4.0 beta 1 — Horus

**This is a beta, for testers.** It is signed and notarized like a release, but Kannu's automatic
updates never offer it: you get it only by downloading the DMG below. When 1.4.0 ships, your Kannu
updates itself to it as usual.

Requires macOS 14.6 or later. It installs over 1.3.4 and keeps your settings.

### Please try

- **Brain › Tasks**: turn on **Enable tasks**, then **Add a source** — GitHub (or your company's
  GitHub Enterprise Server), Linear, Todoist with your own tokens, and Apple Reminders (macOS asks
  once). Jira and GitLab connections from 1.3.4 should keep working without connecting again.
- **The timer with an agent working**: the traffic light should sit in the middle of the timer,
  below the notch on a MacBook. Press Caps Lock during a timer: a short flash, then the timer.
- **A monitor without a notch** with "Always show" off: the notch stays out while a timer runs and
  tucks away when it stops; resting the pointer on the top edge brings it back.
- **The Usage tab**: the Claude card's ↻, Codex's limits, Cursor's Auto and API bars.
- **Keychain**: if macOS asks whether Kannu may use a saved token, note which one and when.

### Going back

Please stay on the beta until 1.4.0 ships. This beta upgrades Kannu's agent hook and adds new
Keychain items, and going back to 1.3.4 over them has not been tested; if you have to, tell David
first.

### Reporting

Send David what you did, what you expected, what happened, the time, and a screenshot if you can.

### Tasks, in Brain

**Brain › Tasks** is new, and off until you turn on **Enable tasks**.

- **One place for every task.** Your own tasks sit beside the ones from the sources you connect,
  as a tree: Source › Project › Task. **All tasks** lists everything at once, with **Find** (title,
  key, tag or label) and filters for source, project, status and tag.
- **Estimates you can type.** "45", "90m", "1.5h", "2d 3h" — a day is a workday of 8 hours — and
  the presets are still one click away.
- **Tags with colours.** Add tags one at a time, pick a colour for each, and drag them into order:
  the first tag's colour colours the task. A task with no tags takes its source's colour.
- **Reminders.** Schedule one on any task; macOS asks for notification permission the first time.
- **Recently closed, then the Archive.** A task you mark done, or a ticket closed in its source,
  waits in Recently closed for 3 days, then moves to the Archive. Archived tickets clear after 7
  days — never while they hold time you have not logged. Your own tasks stay until you delete them.

### Six sources, read-only

Connect them under **Brain › Tasks › Add a source**:

- **Jira Cloud** and **GitLab** (issues and merge requests).
- **GitHub**, on github.com or your company's **GitHub Enterprise Server**: the open issues
  assigned to you, and your pull requests if you want them.
- **Linear**: the issues assigned to you that are not done.
- **Todoist**: your open tasks, narrowed by a Todoist filter if you like.
- **Apple Reminders**: every incomplete reminder, each list as a project. macOS asks for access
  once, when you connect it, and a reminder you add on your iPhone appears without a refresh.

Kannu only reads these; it never changes a ticket. Tokens stay in your Mac's Keychain, in items
only Kannu can read. **Time to log** is the one exception, and it asks first: time you record on a
Jira or GitLab task waits as a draft, rounded to 15 minutes, until you click **Log**.

### The timer tab is where you work

- **Tasks and Presets, side by side.** The timer tab's right-hand column has two pages; swipe
  anywhere on the tab, or click the labels under it. It opens on Tasks when a source is connected.
- **A task opens into its card.** Click a task: its title, its short key (`ds-vendors #4264`,
  `ABC-123`) coloured by where it stands — yellow to do, green in progress, red closed — its
  labels, the facts that matter (due date, health, weight, parent, checklist, linked merge requests,
  comments) and a **Start** button. Closing it puts you back where you were in the list.
- **New task and Edit task in place.** The pencil beside the session name turns the left side into
  a task form: title, estimate, tags (the ones you already use are offered), a reminder.
- **Search, filter, refresh and Time to log** sit in a small rail between the two sides.
- **Name a session** before it starts, and rename it while it runs. Timing a task names the session
  after the task's title, with its short key as a chip beneath.

### A running timer stays in the notch

- **The agent light no longer pushes the timer out.** While an agent works, its traffic light sits
  in the middle of the running timer — below the notch on a MacBook — instead of replacing it for a
  few seconds after every change.
- **Caps Lock flashes, then gets out of the way** while a timer runs.
- **On a monitor without a notch**, the notch stays out for as long as a timer runs, and tucks away
  when it stops.
- With music playing, the timer badge sits beside the album art, not over it.

### Agent monitoring, steadier

- **A green chat no longer pins the notch out**, and a momentary blip never flickers the light. A
  finished chat's red appears about a second late, on purpose.
- **A chat stays green while the agents it started are still running.**
- **Kannu stays light with several agents at once.**
- **The hooks follow each agent's own contract.** Cursor chats no longer show up as Claude Code
  sessions; Cursor's new session and subagent events, Claude Code's rate-limit stop, and Codex's
  hook trust are all handled; Antigravity's hooks are written in the shape Antigravity documents.
  Kannu upgrades its hook script itself.

### Usage, current again

- **The Claude card's Refresh fetches again**, and if Claude Code is not signed in to claude.ai, a
  press opens the sign-in in your browser and fetches when it is done. The card follows the
  organization Claude Desktop is on, and says how old a reading is.
- **Codex**: limits come from Codex itself — `codex app-server` when you open the Usage tab, or
  the newest session log otherwise — so Kannu no longer reads Codex's saved login token for them.
  Token totals count real requests, and a Keychain prompt only ever follows a press of the card's
  button.
- **Cursor**: chat names come back, and the card splits its **Auto** and **API** pools under
  **Billing cycle**.
- **Antigravity** shows its model quotas when its CLI reports them.
- **Memory**: reading token usage no longer climbs past a gigabyte; Claude transcripts and Codex
  session logs are read once, then only what was appended.

### Settings is now Brain

The brain icon in the notch opens it, and so does ⌘,. **Productivity** (Timer, Notes, Tasks) sits
right under AI Agents.

### Fullscreen video hides the notch again

With "Hide only when NowPlaying app is in fullscreen", the notch hides once the video plays and
comes back when you leave fullscreen — including when you went fullscreen before pressing play. It
works without Accessibility access too; **Brain › Media** offers the grant for exact detection.

### Privacy

- **Clipboard history skips** copies their app marks private and copied access tokens.
- **Saved secrets** get a fresh Keychain item that only Kannu can read.
- **ADR**: install lines pin one upstream commit, and Kannu reads ADR's new forbidden-asset finding.

### What changes when you update

- **Settings is called Brain** everywhere you see it.
- **Kannu's agent hook script upgrades itself** on first launch; your own hooks are left alone.
- **Reminders and Notifications** are asked for only when you first use them.
- **New text is in English** in every language until the translations catch up.

### Also fixed

- The timer's Hours / Minutes / Seconds always show whole.
- A stopped timer no longer ends the next session, and the alarm stops when a new one starts.
- A finished Clock timer closes with its ease again, and only the session that finished is ended.
- A window rebuild no longer leaves the notch unable to close or scroll.
- Every icon-only control in the notch names itself in a tooltip, and the header's fixed buttons
  keep their place.

Everything else is unchanged.
